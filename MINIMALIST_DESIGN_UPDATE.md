## 🎨 Design Philosophy

### Minimalist Principles
1. **Clean & Professional** - Corporate-friendly colors
2. **High Contrast** - Better readability
3. **Modern** - Contemporary tech aesthetic
4. **Calming** - Blue/teal tones are less aggressive
5. **Versatile** - Works in any business context

### Visual Hierarchy
- **Dark Background** - Reduces eye strain
- **Blue Accents** - Trust and professionalism
- **Teal Highlights** - Modern tech feel
- **White Text** - Maximum contrast
- **Subtle Shadows** - Depth without distraction
